# Four-Year Degree Plan Activity

A degree plan makes your academic direction tangible. It transforms your POV Statement, career research, and SMART goals from ideas into a structured roadmap. This draft is your starting point — it will evolve, and that is expected.

---

## Step 1 — Gather Your Materials

Before building your plan, collect the following:

- Your institution's academic catalog (for course listings and requirements)
- Your degree audit (from your student portal)
- Your SMART Goals Table
- Notes on your preferred major(s)

---

## Step 4 — Plan Your Electives Intentionally

Based on your Career-to-Degree Map, identify 4–6 elective courses you would like to take that support your career direction. These might include:

- Courses toward a minor or certificate
- Skill-building courses identified in your career research
- Courses that explore adjacent interests

**My Intentional Electives:**

1. CYBR 465 — Cybersecurity for Network-Controlled Systems: explore protection of connected infrastructure; cybersecurity TOPS option.
2. CYBR 470 — Cybercrime and Digital Law Enforcement: develop understanding of digital investigations and legal issues; cybersecurity TOPS option.
3. INSC 384 — Database Design: strengthen data organization and SQL skills; AI TOPS option.

## Step 6 — Reflect

Write a short reflection (8–12 sentences):

- What was most difficult about building this draft?
- What gaps or uncertainties did you notice?
- How does this plan reflect your POV Statement and SMART Goals?
- What do you most want to discuss with your academic advisor when you meet?

**My Reflection:**
The hardest part of putting together this degree plan was figuring out how to fit all the courses I want to take into a schedule that makes sense. My uncertainty is whether or not the inclusion of Applied AI along with Applied Cybersecurity will add to the course load. The prerequisites of courses should be taken into account as well. Generally, the program aligns with my POV statement in that it includes cybersecurity as a major area and allows me to develop additional skills in other technology branches. At the same time, the program aligns with my SMART Goals as it provides me with a more specific way to achieve my educational and career goals. I have chosen such courses as they provide me with the opportunities to pursue my professional goals. The organization of courses by semesters has helped me to realize how my education is aligned with career goals. I am interested in Applied AI as well as it seems to me that it may be useful for my career. In my meetings with my academic advisor, the issues I will be interested in discussing are whether it is realistic to combine both fields without increasing the graduation timeline and whether my remaining electives can be used toward both areas.
