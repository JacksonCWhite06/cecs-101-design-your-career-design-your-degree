# Career Vision Statement Activity

This activity guides you through a structured writing process to develop a meaningful Career Vision Statement. You will build it in layers — starting with raw material from your previous work, developing a draft, refining it, and then committing to a final version.

---

## Step 1 — Gather Your Raw Material

Before writing, pull together the following from your previous work:

- Your Personal Empathy Map insights
- Your SWOT Analysis
- Your Personal POV Statement
- Your RIASEC Profile
- Your Career Exploration Map

---

## Step 2 — Answer the Foundation Questions

**What kind of work do I want to do?**
*(Think about tasks, not job titles. What activities do you want to fill your professional days with? What problems do you want to work on?)*
I want to work with technology to solve problems, identify security risks, and protect computer systems and information. I am also interested in using artificial intelligence alongside cybersecurity to solve more complex problems.

**Who do I want to serve or impact?**
*(Who are the people, communities, or sectors your work will benefit? Be as specific as you can.)*
I want my work to benefit organizations and the people who depend on their technology and information being secure. I am especially interested in working in the private sector where cybersecurity is important to protecting systems, data, and everyday operations.
**What values do I want my career to embody?**
I want my career to reflect responsibility, continuous learning, problem solving, teamwork, and adaptability. Since technology and cybersecurity are constantly changing, I also value being willing to continue developing new skills throughout my career.
**What kind of professional do I want to become?**
*(What qualities, character traits, or reputation do you want to be known for in your field?)*
I want to become a knowledgeable and dependable cybersecurity professional who can solve technical problems while also communicating and working effectively with others. I want to be someone who continues learning and can adapt as cybersecurity and AI technologies change.
**What does success look like for me — not financially, but meaningfully?**
*(Set salary aside for a moment. What would make you feel that your career was truly worthwhile?)*
Success would mean building a career where I can use my skills to solve real problems and protect systems and information from security threats. I would also like to continue expanding my abilities and eventually be able to combine my knowledge of cybersecurity and artificial intelligence.
---

## Step 3 — Draft Your Career Vision Statement

Using your foundation questions as raw material, write a first draft of your Career Vision Statement. Aim for 3–5 sentences. Use the structure below as a guide — but feel free to depart from it once you have a draft:

> "My career vision is to [type of work] for/with [who you serve], so that [impact or outcome you want to create]. I want to be known as a professional who [qualities/values]. I see myself building a career in [field or sector] where I can [specific contribution], grounded in my commitment to [core value]."

**My Draft Career Vision Statement:**
My career vision is to work in cybersecurity solving technical problems and protecting the systems and information that organizations and people depend on. I want to become a knowledgeable and dependable professional who is able to adapt to new technologies and continue developing my skills. I see myself building a career in cybersecurity where I can also use my interest and knowledge in artificial intelligence to approach new security challenges.
---

## Step 4 — Refine and Finalize

Read your draft aloud (or read it slowly in your head). Ask yourself:

- Does this sound like me, or does it sound like what I think I should say?
- Is it specific enough to be meaningful?
- Is it broad enough to allow for growth?
- Does it excite me when I read it?

Revise as needed and write your final version below.

**My Final Career Vision Statement:**

My career vision is to build a career in cybersecurity where I can solve real-world technical problems and help protect important systems and information. I want to become a knowledgeable, dependable, and adaptable professional who continues developing new skills as technology changes. I hope to combine my interests in cybersecurity and artificial intelligence to address new security challenges while making a meaningful contribution to the organizations and people who depend on secure technology.
---

💡 **Note:** Your Career Vision Statement is a living document. You are not locked into it forever. You will revisit and revise it in Module 5 and beyond as your self-knowledge deepens and your career exploration continues.
